Refund Policy

Last updated: 3 July 2026

Failed operations

If a paid operation fails on our side, the credits it consumed are refunded to your balance automatically — you don't need to ask.

Unused credits

Unused credits can be refunded to your original payment method within 7 days of purchase. Contact us with the email you signed in with; refunds are processed through Razorpay within 5–7 working days.

Used credits

Credits already spent on completed operations are not refundable — the AI cost is incurred the moment the operation runs.
