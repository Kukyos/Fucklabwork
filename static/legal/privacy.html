Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 July 2026

What we store

  • Account: your email address (used for sign-in codes) and your credit balance/transaction ledger, stored in Supabase.
  • Payments: Razorpay order and payment IDs. Card/UPI details go directly to Razorpay — we never see them.

What we don't store

Your documents. Uploaded .docx files are processed in memory and returned to you; they are not written to disk on our servers or kept after the request finishes.

Third parties

AI features send the relevant text (and, for screenshot patching, embedded images) to Google's Gemini API for processing. Payments are processed by Razorpay. Hosting is provided by Vercel. Each has its own privacy policy.

Your data, your call

Want your account and ledger deleted? Contact us and it's done within 7 days.
