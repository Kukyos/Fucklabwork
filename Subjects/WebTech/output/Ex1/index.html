About the Community

Respawn Point started in 2023 as a small Discord server for a few of us who kept getting split into different lobbies. It has grown into a community of a few thousand people across battle royale, racing, MMORPG and FPS games who mostly just want to play with people who won't rage quit on them.

Why we exist

Public matchmaking is fine until your teammate disconnects at the worst possible moment. We're here so that doesn't have to happen every single match.

Community Roles

Member
Default role once you join. Access to general chat and the game-specific channels.
Moderator
Keeps the chat civil, handles reports, can mute or kick when needed.
Admin
Runs events, manages roles, and is who you complain to about the ping tax on Warden's Keep servers.

How to Join

  1. Read the rules pinned in #welcome
  2. Pick your main game(s):
    1. Battle royale squad
    2. Racing crew
    3. MMORPG guild
  3. Introduce yourself in #introductions
  4. Grab your game roles from the reaction board

Ground Rules

  • Be respectful, no harassment or hate speech
    • this includes chat, voice channels and in-game lobbies
  • No spamming or unsolicited DMs
  • Keep NSFW content out of every channel
  • Follow game-specific etiquette:
    • no griefing teammates
    • no smurfing in ranked lobbies
