Diary Entries

Ooty - my favourite so far

We went up to Ooty during the semester break. It was really cold in the morning and the tea gardens looked very green, almost fake. The bus ride up the hills made me a little sick but it was worth it.

We stopped at the park after the garden and someone was selling bubble wands, so obviously I had to try the giant one. Also ended up messing around with a friend before the bus back.

Kochi

Kochi was a short one, mostly just walking around Fort Kochi and eating a lot of seafood. Did not expect the backwaters to be that calm, it was like glass.

Munnar

This trip almost got cancelled postponed because of rain but we still went. Tea plantations everywhere you look, and it was freezing at night. Definitely going back someday.

Things I always pack

  • Power bank
  • A jacket even if it says it will not be cold
  • Snacks for the bus
  • My notebook to write the diary entries in

How I plan a trip

  1. Pick the place and check the weather
  2. Book the bus or train tickets
  3. Make a small packing list
  4. Go, and write about it after
